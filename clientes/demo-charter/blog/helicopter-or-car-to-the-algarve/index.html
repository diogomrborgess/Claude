Helicopter or car to the Algarve: when each one makes sense

28 October 20264 min read

The A2 motorway is a good road, and for many trips a chauffeured car is the right answer. The helicopter earns its place when the hours matter more than the price, or when the journey itself is part of the stay.

The time, side by side

  1. Lisbon to the Algarve

    By car 2 h 30

    By helicopter 1 h

    About 1 h 30 saved each way

Indicative times, door to door excluded. Driving times assume normal traffic; flight times depend on aircraft, weather and landing site.

Add the drive to the heliport in Lisbon and the short transfer at the other end, and the real saving is usually a little over an hour each way. On a summer Friday, when the motorway slows near the coast, it is more.

When the helicopter is the better choice

  • A day trip: lunch or a round of golf in the Algarve and back in Lisbon for dinner.
  • A connection: you land in Lisbon on a long flight and do not want two more hours on the road.
  • An occasion: the flight along the Alentejo coast is one of the best views in the country.

When the car is the better choice

  • Luggage: a helicopter cabin takes one soft bag per person. Golf bags and large cases go by road either way.
  • Groups of more than six: two helicopters cost more than one comfortable van.
  • Uncertain weather: low cloud or strong wind can delay a flight. A car leaves on time.

What most clients do

Many combine the two. Passengers fly, the luggage leaves earlier by car and is in the room when they arrive. If the weather closes in, the same car is the backup. Ask for both prices in one quote and decide with the numbers in front of you.
