Planning transfers for a wedding in Portugal

30 September 20265 min read

Transport is the part of a wedding guests notice only when it goes wrong. Most venues in Sintra, the Alentejo and the Douro are at the end of a narrow road, far from the hotels. A plan made early keeps the day on time.

Three months before

  1. Count guests by hotel, not in total. The number of pickup points decides the number of vehicles.
  2. Ask the venue about access: gate width, turning space, and whether a minibus can reach the door.
  3. If you want a helicopter arrival, start now. The landing site needs a survey and an authorisation.

One month before

  1. Collect flight numbers for guests arriving from abroad and group the airport pickups.
  2. Fix the shuttle times and print them on the invitation insert or the wedding website.
  3. Plan two returns at night: one after dinner for families, one at the end.

On the day

  1. One coordinator, with a phone and the full list, stands at the main pickup point.
  2. The first shuttle leaves early. A guest who waits ten minutes at the venue is happier than one who misses the ceremony.
  3. Keep one car free all evening for whatever was not planned.

The couple

A car, a classic, a boat across the river or a helicopter: choose it for the photographs and the mood, and let us check that it works with the venue and the timetable. For a helicopter, always keep a car ready in case the weather changes.
