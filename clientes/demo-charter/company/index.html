A small team that plans the whole journey

Based in Lisbon, working across Portugal and into Europe.

Who we are

Cabo Raso arranges private travel in Portugal: jets, helicopters, yachts and chauffeured cars. We do not sell seats or packages. Each trip is planned for one client, by people who know the airfields, marinas and roads involved.

How we work

You deal with one person. That person chooses the operator, checks the aircraft or vessel, and stays reachable until you arrive. Prices are fixed in writing before you confirm, and the operator is always named.

When the weather or a schedule changes, we call you with an alternative already arranged.
