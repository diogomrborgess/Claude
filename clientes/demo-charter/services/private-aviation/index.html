Private jet charter

You choose the departure time and the airport. We find the right aircraft, handle the paperwork and meet you at the terminal.

Who it is for

  • Executives with meetings in two cities on the same day
  • Families and groups travelling with children, pets or sports equipment
  • Hotels and concierges arranging arrivals for their guests
  • Golf and tourism operators moving small groups between regions

What is included

  • Aircraft, crew, fuel and standard catering
  • Airport, handling and passenger fees, itemised in the quote
  • Private terminal access where the airport has one
  • One contact person from request to landing
  • Ground transfer at both ends on request

Common questions

How far ahead do I need to book?

A few days is comfortable. For urgent trips we can often arrange a departure within hours, subject to aircraft and crew availability and airport slots.

How early should I arrive at the airport?

Usually 20 to 30 minutes before departure at a private terminal. We confirm the exact time with your booking.

Can I bring pets or oversized luggage?

In most cases, yes. Tell us in the request so we choose an aircraft with the right hold and check the entry rules of the destination.

Who operates the flight?

Every flight is operated by a licensed air carrier holding an Air Operator Certificate. The operator name and certificate number are stated in your quote and contract.

Tell us where and when

We reply with options and a fixed price, usually within two hours.
