Helicopter flights

The shortest line between two places in Portugal, landing close to where you are going.

Who it is for

  • Travellers connecting from a jet or an airline flight to a resort or estate
  • Couples and planners who want an arrival or departure by air
  • Golf groups playing courses in two regions in one stay
  • Guests who want to see the coast, the Douro or Lisbon from above

What is included

  • Helicopter, pilot and fuel
  • Landing site check and permissions
  • Safety briefing before boarding
  • Weather watch, with an alternative by road if the flight cannot go
  • Luggage sent ahead by car when it exceeds the cabin limit

Three ways to use it

Transfers
Airport to resort, city to estate, one region to the next. We plan around your flight or meeting.
Scenic flights
Lisbon and the Tagus, the Arrábida coast, the Douro terraces. Routes from 20 minutes.
Events and weddings
Arrivals, departures and guest shuttles, agreed with the venue and timed with the programme.

Common questions

Where can a helicopter land?

At airports, heliports and many private sites, with the owner’s consent and the required authorisation. Send us the address and we check it for you.

What happens in bad weather?

The pilot decides whether the flight is safe. If it is not, we move the time or switch you to a car, and you pay only for what was used.

How much luggage can I take?

Usually one soft bag per passenger. Larger cases and golf bags travel ahead by car so they are waiting when you land.

Can you land at a wedding venue?

Often, yes. We visit or survey the site, speak to the venue and handle the authorisation. Allow at least three weeks.

Tell us where and when

We reply with options and a fixed price, usually within two hours.
