VIP transfers

A driver who already knows your flight number, your name and where you are going.

Who it is for

  • Passengers arriving on private or commercial flights
  • Executives with a day of meetings across the city
  • Hotels and concierges booking on behalf of guests
  • Event and wedding planners moving guests on a schedule

What is included

  • Professional chauffeur who speaks English and Portuguese
  • Flight tracking and waiting time after landing
  • Meet and greet in arrivals or at the aircraft steps where permitted
  • Water, Wi-Fi and phone chargers on board
  • Tolls, fuel and parking in the price

Three ways to book

Airport pickups
Lisbon, Cascais, Porto, Faro and Beja, to your hotel, home or next flight.
Chauffeur by the hour
A car and driver at your disposal for meetings, shopping or a day in the wine country.
Events
Several vehicles on one plan, with a coordinator on site for weddings and corporate events.

Common questions

What if my flight is delayed?

We track the flight and adjust the pickup. Waiting time after the actual landing is included.

Do you provide child seats?

Yes, at no charge. Tell us the ages when you book.

Can I book for someone else?

Yes. Hotels, assistants and planners book for guests every day. The passenger receives the driver’s name and number.

Do you cover long distances?

Yes, anywhere in mainland Portugal and into Spain, with a second driver when the day requires it.

Tell us where and when

We reply with options and a fixed price, usually within two hours.
