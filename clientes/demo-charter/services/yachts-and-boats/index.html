Yacht and boat charter

A day on the water with a skipper and crew, from a quiet anchorage in Arrábida to dinner off Cascais.

Who it is for

  • Families and friends who want a private day at sea
  • Companies hosting clients or a small team on board
  • Hotels and concierges adding a day on the water to a stay
  • Planners arranging arrivals by sea for a wedding or event

What is included

  • Yacht or boat, skipper and crew
  • Fuel for the agreed itinerary
  • Safety equipment and passenger insurance
  • Water, soft drinks and towels
  • Catering, chef or water toys on request

Where we sail

Lisbon and Cascais
The Tagus at sunset, the Cascais coast, lunch at anchor.
Arrábida and Tróia
Clear water, sheltered coves and dolphins in the Sado estuary.
Algarve
Caves and cliffs between Lagos and Albufeira, from Vilamoura or Portimão.

Common questions

Do I need a licence or sailing experience?

No. Every charter comes with a professional skipper, and crew where the vessel requires it.

What if the sea is too rough?

The skipper decides on the day. We offer a sheltered itinerary or a new date at no extra charge.

Can we eat on board?

Yes. We arrange catering or a chef, or book a table at a restaurant you reach by tender.

Can the day be combined with a flight or transfer?

Yes. A car or helicopter can take you to the marina and back, on a single booking.

Tell us where and when

We reply with options and a fixed price, usually within two hours.
